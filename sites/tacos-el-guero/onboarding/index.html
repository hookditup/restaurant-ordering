Texting setup survey

Phone carriers require every business that sends texts to register first. Your answers let us register your restaurant so texts like order updates, reservation reminders and rewards reach your guests instead of being blocked. It takes about 10 minutes.

Fields marked *with an asterisk are required. Business details must match your IRS records exactly, or carriers reject the registration.
