Privacy Policy

Tacos El Guero 2 LLC, doing business as Tacos El Güero, operates this website.

What we collect

We collect what you enter on our site, such as your name, email, phone number, order details and job applications, and basic site usage data. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

How we use it

To prepare your order, contact you about it, run our rewards and gift cards, answer questions and improve our service. If you opt in, we send marketing texts or emails. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their marketing.

Text messages and email

Reply STOP to cancel texts or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
