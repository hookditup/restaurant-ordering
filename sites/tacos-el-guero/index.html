Food that Brings Happiness!

Fiery Flavor Fusion

Immerse yourself in the tender embrace of our seasoned and grilled Asada, delivering an explosion of savory goodness in every bite. Prepared with care and precision, our Asada will transport your taste buds to a realm of culinary delight. For those who crave a little heat, our nachos come with a kick! Jalapeños and our signature spicy salsa add just the right amount of fiery excitement to tantalize your taste buds.

What sets our Tacos Al Pastor apart?

Our Tacos Al Pastor feature thinly sliced, marinated pork that's been expertly seasoned with a blend of traditional spices, including achiote, garlic, and pineapple. The result? An explosion of flavors in every juicy bite! The sweet, caramelized goodness of pineapple that's grilled to perfection, adding a delightful contrast to the savory pork. It's a fiesta for your taste buds!

Crispy, Flavorful, and Unforgettable.

We believe in quality ingredients. Our Chimichangas are made with the freshest produce, premium meats, and authentic spices, providing a wholesome and delicious dining experience. Whether you're a meat lover, vegetarian, or somewhere in between, our diverse Chimichanga menu has something for everyone.

Sizzling Sensation!

Succulent, seasoned beef or lamb, slow-cooked to perfection until it's fall-apart tender. The result? A taco experience that's not just a meal but a culinary journey. The crispy edges, the juicy center, and the tantalizing aroma will transport you straight to the heart of Mexico.

It's All About Family

Jose & Selena started off as a husband and wife team running a local food truck at farmer’s markets. Shortly after, they opened their first restaurant in the heart of Heber City, which outgrew itself quickly. This resulted in the purchase of a larger location where they are now located at.

Our story

Generations in The Making

Creating a future for their family was the mission behind providing outstanding, authentic Mexican food to the Heber Valley. Jose & Selena’s passion to serve their community while also providing for their family is the heart behind Tacos El Güero.

What meals do you serve?

We serve breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Breakfast is served all day.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

Yes, we offer takeout. Order online for pickup or call us at (435) 654-2983.
