Common questions

What are you known for?

We are known for Chimichanga, Breakfast, Breakfast Burrito, Tacos, Tex Mex, Chicken Tenders, Churros, Ceviche, Carne Asada, Guacamole, Milanesa, Quesadilla, Tortas, Menudo, Seafood, Latin America Food, Mexican Rice, Flour Tortillas, Quesabirria Tacos, Birria Burritos, Cheeseburger, Burritos, Fast Food, Eggs, Huevos Rancheros, Fries, Carnitas, Chilaquiles, Enchiladas, Nachos, Taco Salad, Pozole, Flan, Birria, Quesabirria, Ceviche Tostada, and Tostadas.

What meals do you serve?

We serve breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Breakfast is served all day.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

Yes, we offer takeout. Order online for pickup or call us at (435) 654-2983.

Where are you located?

We are located in Heber City, UT at 281 N Main St (opens Google Maps in a new tab).

What areas do you serve?

We serve the following areas: Heber City, Heber, Daniel, Midway, Center Creek, Interlaken, Charleston, Mound City, Jordanelle, Hailstone, Cranmer, Keetley, Wallsburg, Timber Lakes, Hideout, Independence, Francis, Park City, and Woodland.

Anything else?

We are also known for being a family friendly restaurant.
