A Mother-Daughter Kitchen

Our Story

We're Lupita and Carla Cruz-Calderón. In 2021, we closed our previous restaurant, Tarahumara, and reopened as Lupita's Valley — a chance to dig deeper into the flavors and traditions that define our family's roots.

Read Our Story in the Park Record

Recipes Rebuilt From Memory

Our menu is rooted in Michoacán. The Enchiladas Catalinas are named after a woman from Lupita's hometown. The mole micha-poblano bridges two regional traditions in one deeply layered sauce.

Explore Our Menus

Old Town Square, Heart and Soul

The walls of our dining room feature original paintings by Neyda de la Peña — each one available for purchase. It's a gathering place as much as a restaurant.
