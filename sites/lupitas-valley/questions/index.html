Common questions

What are you known for?

We are known for Camarones A La Diabla, Burritos, Brunch, Quesadillas, Huevos Rancheros, Carnitas, Rice Bowl, Pozole, Nachos, Quesabirria, Bar Food, Grilled Chicken, Tacos, Ceviche, Chilaquiles, Enchiladas, Mole, Omelette, Soup, Tostadas, Tostadas De Tinga, Guacamole, and Fajitas.

What meals do you serve?

We serve Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, and Dinner.

Do you offer takeout?

Yes. Order online for pickup or call us at (435) 654-3465.

Are reservations needed?

We highly recommend making a reservation for groups over 6 people. Request a reservation.

Where are you located?

We are located in Old Town Square in Heber City, UT at 650 W 100 S #10.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the following areas: Heber City, Heber, Midway, Daniel, Charleston, Interlaken, Mound City, Center Creek, Jordanelle, Hailstone, Cranmer, Wallsburg, Keetley, Timber Lakes, Independence, Hideout, Park City, and Francis.
